## How I Work

I enjoy projects that combine research with software engineering, particularly those involving technical leadership, software architecture and long-lived systems.

My approach is to understand the problem first, design the system second and
write code last. Good software is rarely the result of clever implementation
alone; it comes from making sound architectural decisions early and
communicating those decisions clearly.

I am particularly interested in projects where software becomes shared research
infrastructure rather than a one-off deliverable.

## Research and Software

I see research and software as complementary activities rather than separate
disciplines.

Some projects produce publications, others produce reusable software,
datasets or technical infrastructure. In practice these are different outputs
of the same process: understanding complex problems and designing systems that
help other people solve them.

This perspective influences both the software I build and the research I
pursue.

## Collaboration

I enjoy working with multidisciplinary teams where software plays a central
role in enabling research.

Projects are most successful when engineers and researchers work together from
the outset, combining domain expertise with sustainable engineering practices.

I am particularly interested in collaborations involving research
infrastructure, computational methods and open scientific software.
