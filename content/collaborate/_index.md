## Who I Work With

I enjoy collaborating with researchers, engineers and technical leads on
projects where software plays a central role in enabling research.

## Where I Add Value

I typically contribute by designing software architecture, building reusable
infrastructure, improving engineering practices and helping teams turn research
requirements into maintainable systems.

## What Good Collaboration Looks Like

The most successful projects usually begin early, before the software
architecture has been fixed. I work best when engineering decisions are treated
as part of the research process rather than a separate implementation task.
